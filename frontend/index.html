Clothes made for you, not the rack.

Lucky Couture is Guntur's premier boutique destination for bespoke custom tailoring and designer ethnic wear. We specialize in bridal lehenga tailoring, hand-embroidered maggam work blouses, designer sarees, chikankari kurtis, and priority custom stitching with precision doorstep fittings.

Our Services & Collections

  • Bespoke Blouse & Dress Stitching — Custom necklines, sleeves, back patterns, and exact inch measurements.
  • Priority Express Stitching — Fast-track tailoring delivery in 24 to 72 hours for urgent wedding and festive events.
  • Ready-to-Wear Boutique Collection — Handcrafted lehengas, party wear dresses, and sarees.
  • Design Inspiration Gallery — Explore hundreds of trending bridal, festive, and modern ethnic outfits.
  • Tailoring & Styling Guides — Master measurement guides, blouse design trends for 2026, and fabric care tips.
